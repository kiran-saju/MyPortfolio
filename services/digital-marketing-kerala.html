Questions people ask

Frequently asked questions

Is digital marketing the same as social media marketing?

Social media marketing is one part of digital marketing. A broader plan can also involve a website, organic search visibility, landing pages, conversion paths and paid campaigns.

Do you offer Meta advertising support?

I can help plan campaign creative and campaign setup as part of an agreed scope. Advertising spend is separate, and results cannot be guaranteed.

Do I need a website before running marketing campaigns?

Not always. A social profile may be enough for some enquiries, but a good website can provide clearer information, professional credibility and a destination for campaigns.

Can I combine marketing with website development?

Yes. The services can be planned together so the website structure, calls to action and promotional content support the same goals.
