Questions people ask

Frequently asked questions

Can you create both posters and reels?

Yes. Social media content creation can include static posters, carousel designs and short-form promotional videos depending on the agreed scope.

Do I need to give you my Instagram password?

No password sharing is preferred. Where available, proper page access through platform business tools is safer than sharing personal login credentials.

Can you manage both Facebook and Instagram?

Content can be planned for both platforms, but account access, publishing responsibilities and campaign management should be agreed before starting.

Will posting regularly guarantee sales?

No. Content can strengthen visibility and trust, but enquiries and sales depend on the offer, audience, competition, budget and follow-through.
