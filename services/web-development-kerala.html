Questions people ask

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a static website or a dynamic website?

If you only need a few service and contact pages, a static site can be a simple option. If you need to manage products, collect structured data or provide accounts and dashboards, a dynamic site is usually more suitable.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Mobile-responsive layouts are part of my website development approach. Final testing also considers the content and features included in your particular project.

Can you handle the domain and hosting setup?

Yes. I can help configure the domain, hosting, HTTPS and deployment, with project accounts kept under the client's control.

Is SEO included in website development?

Basic technical and on-page SEO can be considered during development. Ongoing SEO research, content creation and optimization are separate activities that can be scoped as needed.
