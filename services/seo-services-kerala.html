How I help

Search visibility starts with a useful, accessible website

Search engine optimization is not simply adding a list of keywords to a page. A useful SEO plan looks at what customers search for, whether Google can access the right pages, how clearly the content answers their questions, and whether the site provides a good experience. The work is prioritized to fit the website and business goals.

Technical SEO review

Review indexability, canonical URLs, XML sitemaps, robots directives, page structure, internal links and obvious technical obstacles.

On-page optimization

Improve descriptive page titles, headings, meta descriptions, image alt text and page copy aligned with genuine customer questions.

Google Search Console setup

Support ownership verification, sitemap submission and interpretation of indexing, search queries and performance reports.

Local search foundations

Help clarify the business's service areas and local relevance through useful location-aware content and Google Business Profile guidance where eligible.

Questions people ask

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee the first page of Google?

No ethical SEO freelancer can guarantee a first-page position. Rankings depend on competition, search intent, content, website quality and factors outside any single person's control.

How is SEO different from Google Ads?

SEO focuses on improving organic search visibility. Google Ads purchases advertising placement and uses a separate budget and campaign strategy.

Do you work on websites you did not build?

An SEO review can be scoped for an existing website, subject to access to its content, settings and relevant search tools.

How long does SEO take?

There is no fixed timeline. Crawling and indexing may take days or weeks, while competitive search improvements can require sustained work and measurement.
